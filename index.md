# Privacy Policy for Streak Tracker

**Effective date:** October 20, 2026  
**Last updated:** October 20, 2026

Streak Tracker is provided by Thedsk8. This Privacy Policy explains how the
Streak Tracker mobile application handles information.

## Information handled by the app

Streak Tracker lets you enter and manage information such as:

- Your chosen username
- Streak names, icons, goals, reminder schedules, and progress
- Completion dates, statistics, and earned badges
- To-do titles, scheduled dates, and completion status
- App preferences, including theme and sound settings

## Local storage

This information is stored locally on your device in the app's private storage.
Streak Tracker does not require an account and does not send this information
to servers operated by Thedsk8.

Your device's operating system or backup provider may include app data in a
device backup if backups are enabled. Those backups are controlled by your
device and account settings and are governed by the backup provider's privacy
terms. Thedsk8 cannot access or manage those backups.

## Information we collect

Thedsk8 does not collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising
identifiers, diagnostic data, or location data through Streak Tracker. The app
does not include advertising or third-party analytics services.

## Notifications and device permissions

If you enable streak reminders, Streak Tracker requests permission to display
notifications. On supported devices, the app also uses system capabilities to
restore scheduled reminders after a device restart, wake the device when a
reminder is due, and use vibration for notifications.

These capabilities are used only to provide reminders you configure. You can
disable notification permission through your device settings.

## Sharing and sale of information

Thedsk8 does not sell, rent, or share your app data with advertisers or other
third parties. Streak Tracker does not transmit your streaks, to-dos, profile,
or activity history to Thedsk8.

## Data retention and deletion

Your information remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear
the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Copies held in a device backup may
remain until removed under your device or backup provider's retention settings.

Because Thedsk8 does not receive or maintain your app data, we cannot retrieve,
restore, or remotely delete it.

## Data security

Streak Tracker relies on the security protections provided by your device and
operating system. You are responsible for maintaining appropriate access
controls on your device. No storage method can be guaranteed to be completely
secure.

## Children's privacy

Streak Tracker is intended for a general audience and is not directed to
children under 3. Thedsk8 does not knowingly collect personal information from
children or any other users through the app.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices
change. The updated policy will be posted at this same location with a revised
"Last updated" date.
